Predicting Insurance Claims With GLMs from Scratch

How it was built

Part 1: The Portfolio

A seeded book of motor policies that imitates the French freMTPL2freq data of section 12.5: rating factors, exposure, a hidden claim rate, Poisson claim counts with unobserved heterogeneity, a train/test split and the zero-heavy claim histogram of Figure 12.1(a).

Part 2: Feature Engineering

The simple feature engineering of section 12.5: quantile bins for vehicle and driver age, one-hot categoricals, standardized bonus-malus and log density, fitted on the training policies and applied unchanged to the test policies.

Part 3: Metrics, the Dummy Model and Ridge

The scoring side of section 12.5: Poisson deviance (eq. 12.35-12.37), exposure-weighted MSE and MAE, total predicted claims, the constant-frequency baseline, and ridge regression on the claim frequency with exposure weights.

Part 4: The Poisson GLM from Scratch

Poisson regression with an exposure offset written as a GLM (eq. 12.17-12.22), its gradient and Hessian (eq. 12.29-12.34), the IRLS view of a Newton step, a damped Newton solver, and the multiplicative relativities an actuary reads off the fitted coefficients.

Part 5: Which Model Is Better?

Table 12.1 rebuilt on held-out policies, the calibration plot of Figure 12.2 with exposure-equal bins, Lorenz curves and the Gini index for risk ranking, and a check of the GLM's predicted claim-count distribution against the overdispersed truth.

Part 6: Beyond Frequency: Severity and the Pure Premium

An extension beyond the book's example: simulated claim amounts, a Gamma severity GLM with the non-canonical log link fitted by Fisher scoring (section 12.3), the frequency-times-severity pure premium scored with the Tweedie deviance, and the end-to-end report the scaffold prints.
